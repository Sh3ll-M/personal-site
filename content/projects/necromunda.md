A little browser tool for tabletop Necromunda. After months of games a group
tends to run through all the published scenarios, and coming up with fresh ones
every week gets tough. This rolls up a complete scenario on demand: a
battlefield, a primary objective, a random complication, a few secondary
objectives and the rewards for winning.

It has two modes. Hive War is a plain mode for a quick skirmish, and Underhells
is a themed one with its own rules cards and a running commentary from Vorn
Kadd, the Ferryman of the Underhells. The whole thing is a single static HTML
page with no backend, so playing is just a case of opening the link.

Hit **demo** above to have a roll yourself, or **repo** for the code. There is
a fuller write up in the post below.
